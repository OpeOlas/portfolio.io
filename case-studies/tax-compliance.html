Taxpayer compliance intelligence

Using predictive modelling to support better compliance decisions.

Python · XGBoost · SHAPOpeyemi Olaosebikan

IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy contributionOutput and evidenceGovernance lesson

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Tax compliance teams need to prioritise attention across large taxpayer populations. My MSc research explored how predictive modelling and digital insights could support that decision.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

I developed a research workflow around data preparation, classification and model interpretation. The public repository includes separate notebooks with and without SMOTE, making the treatment of class imbalance visible. Explainability connects a risk score to the factors influencing it.

OUTPUT AND EVIDENCE

Output and evidence

The output is a research project and accompanying notebooks for reviewing the modelling approach. It demonstrates a route towards risk informed prioritisation; it does not establish a production deployment or a measured operational saving.

THE GOVERNANCE LESSON

The governance lesson

A model score needs reliable source data, clear definitions, access controls and human review. Any operational use would require validation on representative data, monitoring and approval by the responsible business owner.
