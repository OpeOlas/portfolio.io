Tax data migration and validation

Putting traceable controls around a complex operational migration.

Validation · Lineage · ReconciliationOpeyemi Olaosebikan

IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy contributionOutput and evidenceGovernance lesson

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Moving tax records between systems creates risks around duplicate identities, missing mandatory fields and changes in meaning. A successful load alone does not establish that the migrated data is usable.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

During my FIRS data management work, I supported TaxProMax migration activities involving more than 40,000 records. My contribution included data validation, duplicate management, metadata and tracking transformations through the migration process.

OUTPUT AND EVIDENCE

Output and evidence

The case illustrates how migration controls connect source preparation, transformation and target validation. My role was to support data integrity and traceability within the programme. Operational datasets and internal documentation are not published here.

THE GOVERNANCE LESSON

The governance lesson

Reconciliation must compare business meaning as well as record counts. A source to target mapping, exception log and accountable sign off give a migration an auditable basis.
