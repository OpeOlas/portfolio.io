Carbon market data governance

Connecting emissions data, accountability and carbon market processes.

Governance · Metadata · Data qualityOpeyemi Olaosebikan

IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy contributionOutput and evidenceGovernance lesson

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Carbon market programmes rely on information moving between organisations and stages of a credit lifecycle. Unclear ownership, inconsistent definitions and fragmented evidence weaken trust in that information.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

At Civic Innovation Lab, my advisory work has covered governance frameworks, data quality assessments, shared definitions and stakeholder enablement for carbon market initiatives. The approach brings ownership and lifecycle controls into the design of data pipelines and platforms.

OUTPUT AND EVIDENCE

Output and evidence

My work includes three governance frameworks and more than ten data quality assessments, alongside workshops and maturity planning. This case describes my governance contribution; it does not claim independent verification or certification of carbon credits.

THE GOVERNANCE LESSON

The governance lesson

Trust requires a traceable evidence chain. Definitions, provenance, access and approval responsibilities should travel with the data through each lifecycle stage.
