Pharmaceutical data governance

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

The PharmCo capstone provided a setting for translating data strategy into working governance arrangements. The challenge was to connect business priorities with definitions, responsibilities and measurable controls.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

I developed a business glossary, critical data element thinking, ownership and stewardship documentation, a quality dashboard, an issue log and a data literacy pack. These components connect the operating model to day to day data management.

OUTPUT AND EVIDENCE

Output and evidence

The result is a capstone governance package showing how definitions, quality measurement and remediation can work together. It is educational project work, not a claim of implementation in a pharmaceutical company.

THE GOVERNANCE LESSON

The governance lesson

Governance artefacts become useful when someone owns the next action. A glossary entry, quality exception and issue record should point to accountable people and a clear workflow.
