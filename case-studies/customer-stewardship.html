Professional experience / Customer data

Customer data quality and stewardship

Keeping customer information reliable in a service environment.

CRM · Duplicates · Issue SLAsOpeyemi Olaosebikan

IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy contributionOutput and evidenceGovernance lesson

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Customer service depends on accurate records and timely correction of issues. Duplicate profiles and inconsistent updates can disrupt service and reduce confidence in CRM information.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

In my Nespresso UK stewardship work, I supported customer record quality, duplicate management, issue resolution and responsible handling of information. I also contributed to data literacy and adherence to agreed processes.

OUTPUT AND EVIDENCE

Output and evidence

My reported role outcomes include 99.5% CRM integrity, a 25% reduction in duplicates and 98% of data quality issues resolved within SLA. These are experience based outcomes; internal customer records and scorecards remain private.

THE GOVERNANCE LESSON

The governance lesson

Quality controls must fit the service workflow. Clear update rules and prompt exception handling make stewardship part of everyday customer support.

About the evidence
