Professional experience / Data quality

Workforce data standardisation

Making workforce information consistent across systems and reporting.

Profiling · CDEs · StewardshipOpeyemi Olaosebikan

IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy contributionOutput and evidenceGovernance lesson

THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Workforce reporting draws on identifiers, organisational structures, grades and dates. Inconsistent formats and conflicting values make apparently simple headcount questions difficult to answer reliably.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

In my Human Capital governance work, I focused on ownership, critical data elements, shared definitions and data quality controls. My standardisation approach checks identifier uniqueness, date validity, reference values and consistency across related fields before prioritising remediation.

OUTPUT AND EVIDENCE

Output and evidence

The work supports a governed workforce data environment covering more than 12,000 staff. The emphasis is on reusable rules, accountable ownership and a clearer basis for workforce reporting, rather than one isolated cleaning exercise.

THE GOVERNANCE LESSON

The governance lesson

A valid value is not always a correct value. Rules must reflect HR meaning, while exceptions need an owner, a resolution and a record of the change.
